### 🍳 Retseplar Dunyosi
*World of Recipes*

![React](https://img.shields.io/badge/React-20232A?style=flat-square&logo=react&logoColor=61DAFB)
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-In%20Development-1f6feb?style=flat-square)

A React recipe application that helps users discover what they can cook based on the ingredients they already have.

**The problem:** people often have ingredients at home but don't know what to cook.

**The idea:** suggest meals, salads, desserts, and other recipes from what the user already has. Made for anyone who cooks at home, including people just starting to cook.

**Planned / in-progress features**
- Recipe recommendations
- Ingredient-based suggestions
- Favorites with a heart button
- Required ingredients list
- Step-by-step cooking instructions
- AI-based recipe suggestion *(concept)*

**Architecture ideas:** `api/recipes.json` · `common/layout` · recipe components · favorites context · hooks · pages · React Router
